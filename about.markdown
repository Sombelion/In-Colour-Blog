Hi! My name is Mike. I'm a 30 year-old guy from Cornwall, now living in London with my partner.

<hr>

In 2024 I started a personal writing project called In Colour. It was a weekly blog and was part of my effort to build a life outside of the career I had slowly become dispirited with.

The name 'In Colour' came from me wanting to experience my life more vibrantly and pay attention to the complexities again during a period of depression, and my love of writing was a way I could slow down and do that. This all started just as I was reaching what turned out to be an absolutely cratering burnout.

Now, my writing has evolved into a wider project -- Mook Writes. In Colour is still a staple of my creative life, but I also create and share other things that interest me.

If you want a weekly dose of introspection, vulnerability, and (what I hope is) thoughtful and interesting writing, consider keeping up with me and [subscribing via RSS]({{baseurl}}/In-Colour-Blog/feed.xml). 

My only goal is to leave you thinking "I'm glad I read that."
