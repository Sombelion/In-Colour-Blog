I finally managed to sell my big fish tank and decided to treat myself to a sun hat. My head is abnormally large so 99% of hats don’t fit me, but I tried on one of those safari–style sun hats with the dangly cord, and amazingly it was just big enough.

I had largely written off headwear because finding a hat that fits was such an impossibility, and something felt tragic about specifically seeking out extra large hats for people with gigantic heads. For a while I wore this hiking cap when I absolutely needed to wear one, but the fabric was so tight it looked like my head was wrapped in cling film or something.

Anyway, this might be a dumb admission but I’m actually surprised at how much cooler I feel. Maybe there’s something to this hat business.

![a hat that fits](\_images\a-hat-that-fits.jpg)

I feel like I’m approaching a transitional point for my writing. I’ve been thinking about In Colour, what it is now, what I want it to be, and what other projects I want to put my energy into. Recently, I’ve also been thinking about Substack and whether it’s definitely the platform I want to use.

When I feel like I’ve hit a natural pause point — maybe after this post, maybe after a couple more — I’m going to take a hiatus from weekly In Colour posts.

It won’t be forever and maybe not even for very long; I just need to do some focused thinking and experimenting around this stuff and it’s difficult to get into that headspace on a weekly writing schedule.

One of the main drivers for me taking this break is that, for a while, I’ve felt myself thinking “I need something to post this week” rather than “I would love to share this”. That’s not like a crime against blogging or anything, and my heart is still very much in what I create, but I would like to get back to a place where I’m drilling into the stuff I really want to say and have energy to write about.

I also think this might be because life has generally gotten busier. I’m trying to dedicate more energy to finding work (on that note, I had a great interview a couple weeks ago that was extremely promising), I’m trying to complete some minor home projects, and me and Caitlin are getting ready to adopt guinea pigs (eeeeeee).

It might be that I take a few weeks off and then decide I want to keep writing weekly blogs for In Colour. Sometimes you just need a break, after all. But maybe it’ll create space for me to try doing something new.

Oh, I also mentioned platforms.

Before I started using Substack, I thought it was basically just a newsletter/email website, but it’s really more of a social media. You follow people, there’s a feed, there are algorithmic elements, and with that comes a lot of annoyances. On the rare occasion I open the homepage, it tends to be full of low-quality engagement bait or weirdly far right stuff. Maybe this is because my algorithm isn’t very tuned to me and I could probably fix that by trying to curate it all a bit more, but to be honest I would rather it wasn’t part of the transaction at all.

I know I don’t have to engage with that side of Substack — I could just write and ignore the rest — but I think there might be better, more stripped-down options out there for what I want to do, including potentially my own website.

In any case, I may share a few more posts but after that I’ll be taking a hiatus. I’m excited to reflect on everything and decide what I want to do next.

Thanks for reading. Maybe see you next Wednesday.

-Mike 💚
